My name is Brent Mattheu Delgado, 20 years of age and i go to Technological University of the Philippines studying Bachelor of Technology and Livelihood Education Major in Information Communications Technology (BTLED-ICT). I've spent more than 3 years going around and exploring the landscape of the University belt to curate an accurate and ubiased list of places to go to when you're looking for food, comfort, and connectivity.

My Top Picks

Top 1: Kadiliman: Esports Stadium

Food, Comfort, And Connectivity all in one. This Esports cafe is located at 659-A, Brgy, Manila Real Residences, 1129 Natividad Lopez St, Ermita, Manila, 1000 Metro Manila

I've been coming here for months and even years but with different branches and locations, but this one is the best. The place is very spacious and has a lot of seating areas, with a very good internet connection. The food is also very good, with a lot of options to choose from. The computers are strong and can handle demanding tasks. The staff are also very friendly and accommodating. The place is also very clean and well-maintained. I highly recommend this place to anyone looking for a good place to eat, chill, and connect with friends.

Top 2: Cafe Siriusdan

When Good Coffee meets Great Comfort. 492 Halcon, Extension, Mandaluyong City, 1550 Kalakhang Maynila

This is one of the places you go to and just ponder how did ones mind come up with such artistic idea to create a cultural yet modern cafe. The place is very spacious and has a lot of seating areas, with a average internet connection. The food is also very good, with a lot of options to choose from, like pasta and pasteries, hot meals and sweets. One thing that sets them apart from other coffee shops is they have so many unique art pieces and paintings that you can take pictures with. this is defitnetly a place you wouldn't want to miss out on.

Top 3: WareHouse Coffee

When Good Coffee meets Great Comfort. 492 Halcon, Extension, Mandaluyong City, 1550 Kalakhang Maynila

This is a cozy and inviting coffee shop that offers a perfect blend of quality coffee and comfortable seating. The atmosphere is relaxed and ideal for studying or working. The food options are diverse, with a focus on fresh and healthy choices. The staff is knowledgeable and friendly, making every visit a pleasant experience. I highly recommend this place to anyone looking for a great coffee shop with a welcoming vibe.
